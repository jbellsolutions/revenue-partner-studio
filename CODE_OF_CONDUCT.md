# Code of Conduct

Orgo AI Guy Bot is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community.

Contributors, maintainers, and community members must:

- communicate respectfully and assume good intent while evaluating technical claims on evidence;
- welcome people regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability;
- keep feedback focused on the work rather than the person;
- avoid harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, discriminatory language, and publishing private information;
- respect maintainers' decisions about project scope and community safety.

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, and other spaces where someone represents Orgo AI Guy Bot.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not include security vulnerabilities in a conduct report; follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, with conflicts of interest routed to a different maintainer when possible.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
